01 / THE THINKINGBEFORE THE BRIEF

Before the ad,
three questions.

Before I brief an ad, I want to understand the person on the other side of it.

01

Do they know the product?

If it’s unfamiliar, the first job might be to explain what it is and why someone would use it.

02

What do they want or doubt?

I look at reviews, comments and search terms for the language, motivations and objections that matter.

03

What are we testing?

A new angle, a different hook, an offer or a format? I want to know what the result can teach us.

THE SYSTEM ↗ I’ve built a set of criteria around these questions to check each concept before it goes live. Once it’s running, I look at what happened and use that to decide what to make next.
