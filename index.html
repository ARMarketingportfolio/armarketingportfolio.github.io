01 / My operating system

Research → diagnosis → roadmap → launch → learning.

I do not treat media buying, creative and reporting as separate jobs. They are one decision system: each stage should make the next decision clearer.

  1. 01

    Diagnose the constraint

    Audit the account, funnel, offer and economics. Define the problem before prescribing more ads.

    Output: a clear strategic diagnosis
  2. 02

    Find the customer language

    Mine reviews, search queries, comments, past winners, competitor ads and landing pages for real signals.

    Output: insight and voice-of-customer library
  3. 03

    Build the roadmap

    Turn research into prioritised personas, angles, offers, hooks, formats and testable hypotheses.

    Output: creative roadmap and backlog
  4. 04

    Connect the funnel

    Align creative, paid acquisition, landing pages, remarketing and lifecycle retention around one journey.

    Output: funnel map and testing plan
  5. 05

    Launch with discipline

    Write briefs and copy, structure campaigns, allocate budgets, coordinate production and run pre-launch QA.

    Output: campaigns the team can execute
  6. 06

    Read the business, not one metric

    Use ROAS alongside revenue, MER, contribution margin, pacing and customer mix—then feed the learning back in.

    Output: the next better decision

02 / The systems behind the work

Useful structure, not strategy theatre.

The goal is to leave the team with a repeatable way to research, create, launch, measure and improve—not a presentation that becomes obsolete next week.

Research system

One source of truth for customer and market insight.

Customer language, use cases, objections, competitor patterns, search intent and historical performance—organised for reuse.

Creative strategy system

A roadmap that explains what to test—and why.

Prioritised hypotheses, angles, hooks, formats, briefs, owners, status and learnings instead of disconnected creative requests.

Commercial decision system

Performance viewed through the P&L.

Budget pacing, forecast versus actual, revenue, gross profit and contribution margin so campaign decisions reflect business reality.

AI operations layer

Manual judgement first. AI second.

Claude Code, agents and MCP workflows accelerate research synthesis, reporting, QA and recurring operations without replacing the thinking.
