03 / How I work

How I get from a problem to a decision.

The exact work changes by account. The order usually does not.

  1. 01

    Find where money is leaking

    I check the account, offer, funnel and economics before recommending more ads.

    What comes out: the problem to solve
  2. 02

    Read what customers actually say

    Reviews, queries, comments, past winners and competitor pages are more useful than invented personas.

    What comes out: research notes
  3. 03

    Write down what we are testing

    Every concept needs an audience, objection, angle and reason for being made.

    What comes out: a prioritised roadmap
  4. 04

    Make the ad and page agree

    The promise in the ad should continue on the landing page, checkout and retention flow.

    What comes out: the page and funnel plan
  5. 05

    Launch it properly

    I handle briefs, copy, campaign structure, budgets and QA so the test is readable afterwards.

    What comes out: live campaigns
  6. 06

    Check whether it made money

    I read platform data with Shopify, the P&L, contribution margin and new versus returning customers.

    What comes out: the next action
